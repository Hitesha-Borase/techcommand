# Tech Command Academy — shared career engine, separate technical tracks

Owner directives: September 19, 2026, including the later DME Academy and guided field/report instructions. One technician has independently recorded `biomedical` and `dme_mobility` pathways. Neither grants authority in the other. This roadmap supersedes any legacy automatic proficiency score as evidence of technical authorization. Compensation, signed records and existing identities remain unchanged. Later user requests to continue cover the entire phased roadmap, not a claim that every phase is delivered in the first release.

## Inspected architecture and implementation plan

## Phase 1 delivery

Biomedical ladder: Explorer, Foundations, Trainee, BMET I, BMET II, Specialty BMET, Advanced Specialty/Imaging, Lead/Senior, HTM Leadership. DME ladder: Explorer, Mobility Foundations, Trainee, DME I, DME II, Manufacturer/Platform Specialist, Advanced Mobility Specialist. Both use specialties, My Career, saved training records, the existing credential table, immutable competency sign-offs and model/task authorization matrix. Every authorization is scoped to the career track, saved equipment family, exact manufacturer/model and task. No wildcard authorizations, title-based permissions or BMET II-to-imaging shortcut. Administrators may record a documented external assessor's trainer appointment; a portal administrator role is not a technical qualification. All other competency sign-offs require a current matching trainer authority and cannot be self-signed.

Biomedical assignment/field screens show Authorized, Supervision required or Not currently authorized. Planning and partial notes remain possible; normal completion is held for missing authorization. Supervised work requires another currently authorized assigned trainer and review of the exact saved per-asset revision. QA-approved biomedical asset work becomes immutable experience, linked to the actual work order/report/asset and authorization snapshot. No retroactive qualifications or automatic title/pay changes.

DME field/report increment: open an existing SpinLife work order → Begin guided service → record one partner check, note/dictation/photo → Save & continue → review AI wording → stored partner/service PDF → Send for admin review. Previously the full partner checklist was expanded at once and applying polished wording overwrote raw narratives. Now all raw entries remain; reviewed wording is a separate evidence-fingerprinted snapshot. Changing facts invalidates its use without erasing it. Recorded/N/A/unfinished are distinct, not automatically passed. PDF generation preserves the administrator review queue, labels incomplete records and uses technician initials + full work-order suffix + status. The same signed-source manufacturer guide remains separate from partner administrative checks. Automatic technical branching, photo diagnosis and a mandatory final administrator invoice/report closeout for the simple SpinLife path are not claimed in this increment.

## Simple DME accounting handoff increment

The simple SpinLife field packet now feeds the existing canonical QA, invoice and payroll path. No second accounting engine or technician re-documentation is introduced. Administrator billing drafts can remain empty; preparation requires saved actual field evidence plus a real bill-to account/location and separately approved price/cost reference. The original source snapshot, raw notes, stored packet, photo captions, signature/attestation times and agreed pay are retained. QA approval remains distinct from preparation and actual payment. Employee paid time is not inferred from starting a job; no wage is changed. Recommended/ordered parts are not documented as installed, and blank N/A reasons do not count as resolved work.

DME experience is connected: supported canonical DME QA approval writes an immutable report-linked record in the same transaction. My DME Career reuses the existing experience table and advancement evaluator. Selected equipment families use the shared DME matrix; missing family remains null without creating equipment/CRM placeholders. Exact manufacturer/model, disposition, original report version and current authorization context are retained. Actual supervision is not inferred from QA; no automatic qualification, title or wage follows. Successful PM/repair repetition requirements exclude pending/failed outcomes; unclassified work does not inflate family breadth. Draft, merely prepared or unreviewed work never earns approved experience. DME revision-bound supervisor review is also connected to the exact report revision.

Administrator-reviewed DME quality outcomes are now separate immutable evidence. Diagnostic resolution, first approved repair, callback status through a recorded date and first-pass documentation each use an explicit denominator. Parts availability, authorization delay, evaluation scope and additional hidden failures remain exclusions rather than technician failures. Quality percentages in advancement policy require a minimum reviewed-outcome count. The living development roadmap presents only configured and verified gaps; it cannot authorize work, advance a title or alter pay.

## Granular DME assignment and supervised-work increment

Implemented in the same `AcademyJobCheck`, Academy authorization matrix, work-order assignment and canonical service-report workflow. Dispatch and active fieldwork evaluate DME family × exact manufacturer/model × task. Cross-track authority, title, level, training and credentials do not substitute. Scheduling and incomplete drafts remain saveable.

For technicians whose reviewed DME Academy profile/authorization program is configured, normal completion requires exact independent/trainer authority or a lower-level authorization plus a different assigned exact trainer. The shared Academy presents that trainer with the actual saved DME report evidence. `academy_report_supervision_reviews` binds the signature to report revision/fingerprint and the current learner/trainer authorization IDs and revisions. Changed evidence or authority invalidates the review without rewriting it. QA records supervised experience only when that signed evidence is still current. Existing unconfigured technicians retain the legacy completion path while management records actual competency; the UI marks the gap and QA retains `not_recorded` supervision instead of inventing independence.

No DME authorization, career profile, job, service report, quality score, threshold or qualification is seeded.

## Governed technical procedure increment

The existing knowledge center now owns separate Biomedical and DME/Mobility technical documents. Incomplete research remains a versioned draft and is invisible to technician fieldwork. A reviewed publication creates an immutable source revision, service-type workflow template and relational measurement requirements with unit, conditions, acceptance criteria and source locator. No manufacturer procedure, tolerance or contact is prefilled.

The existing Biomedical asset and DME manufacturer-guide engines first resolve an exact published track + manufacturer + model + service-type revision. If none exists they retain the existing research path and explicit unavailable/review-required states; near-match models never qualify. A field save pins the exact published revision and complete procedure snapshot. Later source publications and draft edits do not rewrite that history. These records do not grant career competency, task authorization, a completed finding, credential or compensation.

## Later phases / acceptance boundaries

### Shared course library and education sponsorship increment

Implemented: both career tracks now use one searchable Learning Center inside the existing Academy while retaining separate Biomedical and DME course catalogs. Administrators can retain an incomplete course draft, record reviewed lesson sources, add knowledge checks, and publish an immutable revision. Enrollments pin that exact revision so a later edit never rewrites prior learning or history. No placeholder OEM course, external certification or technical specification is seeded.

Technicians open one lesson at a time, keep their own notes and answers, and **Save progress** at any point. Only the learner can attest participation. Passing a course creates a **submitted** Academy training record for the existing independent review; it does not verify itself or grant competency, a credential, title, pay, or service authority. Returned evidence can be corrected and submitted as a new training record without rewriting the returned record. School coordinators see only basic title/progress for linked placements in their authorized school; customer work, answers, sponsorship, compensation and other schools remain excluded.

Education sponsorship now follows draft → submitted → explicit decision → linked verified completion. Known cost, requested/approved company contribution, employee remainder and any actual signed-agreement reference stay distinct. A different owner/operations administrator must make the funding decision. The record does not buy a course, transfer money, alter payroll or invent a repayment agreement. Completion links only to an independently verified training record in the same technician/track.

### Evidence-driven advancement and capability increment

Implemented next: administrators configure per-track target-level policies with named verified training, current exact-name/issuer credentials, QA-approved job/PM/repair counts, family breadth and current practical/task authorizations. Published policies show transparent actual/required progress in My Career. There are no default invented thresholds or pay bands. Draft policy saves remain inactive. Ready technicians appear automatically in the existing Academy management queue; requesting review freezes the policy and evidence. Owner/operations approval changes the Academy level only, with an immutable audit; employment title, wage and service authorizations remain unchanged. Compensation-review status can be handled separately after a career decision, without a payroll write. Technician views are self-scoped and contain no management workforce data.

The same current exact-model/task records drive Workforce capability and Biomedical facility PM coverage. Missing categories/identity or authority are explicit gaps; DME accounts, retired and reference-only inventory are excluded from the Biomedical analysis. Coverage is recorded competence, not a claim of staffing, tools, source approval, contract coverage or completed maintenance. Readiness is an in-app notification, not an email or SMS.

Phase 2 learner portfolio presentation is implemented in the existing My Career / My DME Career Learning Center. The linked learner can download a private PDF that keeps exact pinned course participation separate from independently verified training. It exposes no answers, answer keys, notes, sponsorship/cost/funding, compensation, customer/work-order evidence or other learner; administrators and school coordinators cannot export it on the learner's behalf. The file states that it is not a credential, competency, title, pay decision or service authorization and generation creates no record or audit side effect.

The course/lesson/knowledge-check library, education sponsorship decisions, restricted educator progress view, exact-revision assignment by the student's own authorized school, continuously updated development plans, configured advancement, compensation-review policies and DME-approved-service experience are implemented as described above. School assignment is limited to an active existing placement. No externally recognized credential, repayment agreement or default pay band is invented. Until policy is configured show progress as not configured, not a fabricated percent. Signed-in learner/school acceptance remains unfinished; external portfolio sharing or school attestation is intentionally not inferred from a learner-owned download. Outcome/first-time-fix metrics and DME assignment authorization enforcement remain separate connections; no invented statistics are displayed.

Phase 3: governed immutable manufacturer/model revisions, source-backed structured measurements, exact units, instrument identity and calibration-at-test evidence are implemented as described above. The shared field guide presents one step at a time, displays reviewed photo guidance and can propose a nonnumeric description of positively visible evidence from an exact saved step photo. The proposal is never saved automatically and is barred from diagnosis, procedure-compliance, work-performed, measurement, pass/fail, safety or identifier claims. Source-controlled conditional branches are implemented; broader automated technical photo interpretation remains intentionally unavailable without qualified review. Raw and reviewed technical prose remain separate. Rapid PM scan→identify→authorization→procedure→save→next uses the same work_order_assets records and preserves full selected-asset disposition accounting plus facility master/exception reports. Unmatched model retrieval remains a research candidate or explicit TECHNICAL DOCUMENTATION REVIEW REQUIRED state.

P5-01 field modes are implemented on those same field surfaces. Guided is the safe one-step presentation. Experienced is available only while every selected exact family/model/task check is current and independently authorized; it changes navigation, not requirements. Any learning/observed/assisted/supervised authorization forces Trainee, and the different assigned trainer plus exact saved-revision review remains authoritative. The server recalculates mode availability from the current assignment, discipline, authorization expiry, asset/report identity and Academy epoch; stale selections are displayed as held rather than honored. No course, career level, title, role or saved mode grants technical authority.

Remaining Phase 4: forecasting and opportunity planning. Reviewed quality/callback outcomes and basic exact-task workforce/biomedical PM coverage gaps are implemented above. Do not infer saleable capability from a title, course count, unreviewed service event or inventory alone.

## Acceptance matrix

1. Authorized exact model/task PM → completion and QA-linked experience: Phase 1 regression target.
2. Trainee → qualified assigned supervisor + revision-bound review: implemented and covered by assignment/authorization/result-race regression gates.
3. General title never authorizes C-arm: Phase 1 regression target.
4. Eligible for advancement review + in-app management/technician queue: implemented; policy/evidence race and explicit approval regression gates. External notifications are not sent.
5. Verified credential → configured requirement and compensation-review eligibility: implemented as exact issuer/name evidence in an active target policy; no automatic wage or qualification changes.
6. Facility inventory gaps → exact-model/task management coverage: implemented for Biomedical PM, excluding nonbiomedical/reference/retired records. Forecasting and service-opportunity modeling remain later work.
7. Unverified manufacturer procedure never becomes invented protocol: preserve existing AI validation; strengthen completion/evidence governance in Phase 3.

Browser/physical phone, scanner, microphone and live manufacturer-provider acceptance must be recorded separately from handler/SQLite tests. No independent technician qualification may be seeded merely to make an acceptance test pass in production. Synthetic records only for test activity.
